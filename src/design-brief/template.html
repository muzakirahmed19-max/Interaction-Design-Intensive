BDesign Brief

Background

Company context

Nimbus Labs is a 150-person IT services and product company. The organisation consists of Designers, engineers, QA and DevOps, delivering 10 to 12 concurrent client and internal projects. Projects are short, overlap with each other, and often share specialists such as senior backend developers, DevOps and UX.

Problem statement

Nimbus Labs has no single, reliable view of who is available, what they're skilled at and what they're committed to. As a result, managers make staffing decisions in isolation, which leads to overbooked people, underused talent and missed client deadlines.

Each manager plans resourcing in their own spreadsheet and coordinates over Slack and meetings, which causes the following problems:

  • Visibility problem (fragmented information): managers only see their own team's workload. Nobody can see availability across the organization.
  • Coordination problem (double-booking): the same person gets added to two projects without anyone noticing, because nothing detects conflicts.
  • Communication problem (vague effort terms): "50% available" or "part-time" means different things to different people, which breaks plans.
  • Tooling problem (spreadsheet dependency): shared sheets go stale or fork into multiple versions when several managers edit them.
  • Talent utilization problem (assigning by familiarity): there is no skills directory, so work keeps going to the same 2 or 3 known people while others go unused.
  • Planning problem (short-term and reactive): planning covers only the current sprint, so people are already overbooked by the time the next project is staffed.

This leads to missed client deadlines, uneven workloads, burnout and talent that goes to waste.

The product

A web-based resource allocation platform for IT services organizations. It gives everyone:

One shared view

of each person's availability, skills and project commitments.

Real-time conflict alerts

when someone is overbooked.

Standardized effort

in hours per week instead of vague percentages.

Skill-based search

to find the right person for the work.

A forecast view

of free capacity by role and skill for upcoming weeks and months, so managers can staff projects ahead of time instead of reacting.

Key terms

Allocation
a person assigned to a project for a defined period.
Effort (standardized)
hours per week against a 40-hour week. For example, 50% means 20 hours per week.
Capacity
a person's total available hours after leave and other commitments.
Utilization
allocated hours divided by capacity.
Overbooking or conflict
total allocations above 100% of capacity.
Forecast
projected free capacity by role or skill for a future period.
Skill profile
a person's skills and proficiency levels, kept up to date and searchable.

Secondary

Secondary users

Individual Contributors

(designers, developers, QA, DevOps): keep their skill profile and availability up to date, view their own allocations, and flag when they're overloaded. Their leave comes in automatically from timesheets; the timesheet itself is out of scope.

Admin / Resource Ops

set up roles, permissions and projects, maintain the skills taxonomy and effort standards, and manage integrations such as the timesheet sync and Slack.

Delivery Head / Account Manager

raises staffing needs for new or expanding client engagements, and checks the forecast before committing start dates to clients.

Tertiary

Tertiary users

Leadership (CTO / COO)

views organization-wide utilization, bench strength and delivery risk to make strategic decisions.

Finance

uses billable vs non-billable utilization for revenue forecasting and project costing.

Constraints

  • Web-based. The MVP is designed desktop-first.
  • Must be at least as flexible and familiar as spreadsheets to win adoption.
  • The data is only useful if people keep it updated, so updating has to be low-effort.
GWhy · How · What

Goal

MotivationWhy

To help Nimbus Labs deliver client projects on time with balanced workloads by replacing fragmented, spreadsheet-based staffing with one shared, reliable picture of people's availability, skills and commitments.

ApproachHow

By designing a web-based resource allocation platform that:

  • centralizes availability, skills and allocations in one place
  • standardizes effort in hours per week
  • detects double-booking in real time
  • makes it possible to search for people by skill
  • forecasts free capacity by role and skill for upcoming weeks and months
Measurable targetWhat

Within 6 months of launch, reduce double-booking incidents by 80% and cut the time managers spend on staffing by 50%. At the same time, keep team utilization in a healthy 70–85% range with no one consistently allocated above 100%.

One-line versionHelp Nimbus Labs managers staff projects confidently by building a centralized allocation platform with real-time conflict detection and capacity forecasting, reducing double-booking by 80% and staffing effort by 50% within 6 months.

SMVP boundaries

Scope

In scope

MVP

Users

  • Primary Engineering Manager (Arjun), Project Manager (Nisha)
  • Secondary Individual Contributors, Delivery Head / Account Manager, Admin / Resource Ops
  • Tertiary Leadership (CTO / COO), Finance, with read-only access to utilization

Experiences (user paths)

  1. Viewing availability: see who is free, partly free or fully booked across the organization.
  2. Finding people by skill: search and filter people by skill, proficiency, role and availability.
  3. Allocating people to projects: create, edit, extend and remove allocations in hours per week.
  4. Managing conflicts: get alerted to overbooking and resolve it before confirming.
  5. Forecasting capacity: see free capacity by role and skill for the coming weeks and months.
  6. Monitoring team workload: track utilization and spot overloaded or underused people.
  7. Managing my profile and allocations (Individual Contributors): update skills, view personal commitments and flag overload.
  8. Raising staffing requests (Delivery Head): request people for a new or expanding client engagement and check feasibility.
  9. Basic setup (Admin): manage people, roles, permissions, projects and the skills list.
  10. Viewing organization-wide utilization (Leadership, Finance): see utilization, bench and capacity trends across the organization, read-only.
  11. Onboarding and first-time setup: sign in, complete a profile and learn how effort is measured.

Features

  • Standardized effort in hours per week
  • Real-time conflict detection and alerts
  • Searchable skill profiles
  • Capacity forecast by role and skill
  • Leave synced automatically from the timesheet system

Platform and form factor

  • Web application, designed desktop-first, with a responsive read-only view on tablet and mobile
Out of scope
  • Clients and external contractors as users
  • Building timesheets or time tracking
  • Payroll, billing, invoicing and budgets
  • Hiring and recruitment
  • Performance reviews and appraisals
  • Task-level project management (sprints, tickets)
  • AI-based auto-staffing
  • Full editing on mobile, and native apps
